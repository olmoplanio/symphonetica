Legend

Abbreviations used for manner of articulation, following the reference chart.

  • nas: Nasal (N)
  • stp: Stop & Laryngeal stop (K)
  • afr: Slack stopstrictive (KX)
  • sib afr: Grooved stopstrictive (KS)
  • lat afr: (Uni)lateral stopstrictive (KꞭ)
  • trl afr: Trilled & tapped stopstrictive (K℞)
  • frc: Slack constrictive (X)
  • sib frc: Grooved constrictive (S)
  • lat frc: (Uni)lateral constrictive (Ɬ)
  • trl frc: Constrictive trill (℞)
  • trl: Trill (R)
  • tap: Tap (T)
  • flp: Flap (D)
  • lat: Lateral (L)
  • tla: Tapped lateral (Я)
  • apr: Approximant (J)

Using the Chart

This chart serves as a reference for the Symphonetica phonetic alphabet. Each symbol can be clicked to add it to your transcription work. The symbols are designed to work harmoniously together while maintaining clear distinctions.

For interactive transcription, visit our Transcription Keyboard page, or learn more about the theoretical foundations in our Natural Phonetics section.
